# Designer / Trend-Forecaster / Brand Predictions for Residential Finish & Surface Trends, 2026–2027 (North America, Canada/GTA emphasis)

Research date: 2026-11-04. Scope: what designers, forecasters, paint/surface brands and media say is rising, peaking or over in residential finishes, and which rising finishes have "limewash-2022" characteristics (visual, Instagrammable, hard to DIY, specialist-applied, ~$5k–15k CAD jobs). Facts are in "Cited Findings"; my judgments are in "Inferences". Several high-value pages (Apartment Therapy, Pinterest's own report page, House & Home 2026 issue PDFs, Wolseley Canada release, Marathon Hardware IDS report) returned 403/truncated on fetch; where I relied only on a search snippet rather than the full page, I say so.

---

## Key Question 1 — What do Pinterest, Houzz, Zillow, the US design press and Canadian media identify as rising surface/finish trends for 2026–27?

### Takeaway
Every major 2026 forecast converges on the same cluster: textured/handmade surfaces (textured plaster, limewash, handmade tile, stone), colour drenching, checkerboard floors, warm/dark wood, Art Deco revival, and "wellness" wet rooms. The forecasts are almost entirely consumer-media and search-data driven (Houzz, Zillow, Pinterest); none of them yet name tadelakt or microcement as a headline trend, which is itself a signal that those are earlier on the curve than plaster/limewash.

### Inferences
- The only finish-level trends with hard *search or listing data* behind them for 2026 are checkerboard floors (Houzz +38% / +103% bathroom) and colour drenching (Zillow +149%, H&H 76%). Textured plaster/limewash is named in every designer-opinion list but has no published search-growth figure in any 2026 report I found — consistent with a trend that is designer-led and still pre-mass-market.
- Canadian designer polls (H&H, BC Living) lag the US press by roughly one cycle on *terminology*: Canadian pieces say "textured plaster" and "artisanal tile" generically; the US press already splits that into limewash / Roman clay / tadelakt / microcement. That split is where the niche-service opportunity sits.
- Pinterest Predicts is the wrong instrument for finishes: its home trends are aesthetic "cores," not materials. Houzz and Zillow are better early indicators for surfaces.

## Key Question 2 — What are paint and surface brands pushing, and are there new product categories?

### Takeaway
2027 Colours of the Year across every major brand are earthy greens/browns (Webster Green, Celery, Grounded, Soulful Sage, Country Soil), with brand language leaning on "nature," "grounded," "timeworn" and "mineral pigments" — but I found no evidence of a *new product category* launch (kit tadelakt, kit microcement, etc.) from Romabio, Portola, Bauwerk, Farrow & Ball, Benjamin Moore or Sherwin-Williams in 2026. The mineral/lime category is being sold through existing specialist lines (Portola Lime Wash/Roman Clay, Bauwerk, Romabio Classico, Domingue, Color Atelier), not through the mass brands.

### Inferences
- The mass paint brands are monetising the texture trend via *colour* (earthy greens/browns) and technique (colour drenching, colour capping), not via new mineral products; the mineral/lime category remains a specialist-brand business (Portola, Bauwerk, Domingue, Color Atelier, Romabio, Kalklitir). That keeps application in the hands of decorative painters — good for a specialist-lead marketplace.
- Canada Brick distributing Romabio is a concrete Canadian channel signal for *exterior* limewashed brick, a service with a clear GTA fit (Victorian/Edwardian brick stock).
- The appearance of "tadelakt-effect" kits (Color Atelier, Kreidezeit, Topciment Natture) mirrors the 2022–23 moment when limewash moved from Bauwerk/Portola specialist use into DIY-able products — tadelakt is roughly where limewash was then.

### Gaps
- No press releases found for Kalklitir, Keim, Ideal Work, Fenix or Dekton 2026 trend positioning (not searched individually due to call budget).
- No Benjamin Moore / Sherwin-Williams Canadian colour-of-the-year commentary mentioning textured or mineral finishes was found; their 2027 messaging is colour-only.
- Dulux Canada and Sico 2027 colours not located (AkzoNobel global pick is Soulful Sage; Sico not found).

## Key Question 3 — Which finishes do designers now call tired or "over"?

### Takeaway
Shiplap and board-and-batten are unambiguously declared over (and have been since 2019); the single accent wall is declared over in both the US and Canadian press; fluted panels and *neutral* zellige are contested (some designers calling them dated, retailers still pushing); limewash itself is NOT declared over by anyone I found — in 2026 it is still being presented as the *replacement* for flat paint and shiplap.
