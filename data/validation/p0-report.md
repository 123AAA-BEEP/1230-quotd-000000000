## 5. Competitive landscape (carry into P1/P2 design)

1. Programmatic/EMD networks already operate in these niches (templated "French Drains {City}" multi-metro network; limewash lead-gen sites; findradonpros.com). The tactic is proven; thin execution is the weakness we beat with real data.
2. Franchises (LIME Painting, That 1 Painter, Shack Shine, We Hang Christmas Lights, radon chains) = SEO competitors **and** budgeted lead buyers — first sales calls.
3. Niche directories exist in 4 verticals (NoDig, FindRadonPros, Sauna Marketplace, Hometown Demolition) — the product shape works; none pair it with a local-data layer.
4. Beatability concentrates at the **city × data** layer in 13 of 14 audited niches: platforms own generic heads with un-localized content; city SERPs repeatedly admit thin or junk results. That is precisely the wedge the spec bets on.
