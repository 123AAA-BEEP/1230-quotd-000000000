# SERP-quality audit — batch A (7 niches)

retrieved_at: 2026-08-01
method: 4 WebSearch queries per niche — (1) head term generic, (2) person-form/near-me, (3) cost, (4) city-specific (Toronto). Top ~8 visible organic results classified into: BIG PLATFORM / NATIONAL CONTENT / LOCAL CONTRACTOR / FRANCHISE / PROGRAMMATIC-EMD LEAD-GEN / FORUM-UGC / OTHER. Beatability scored 1–5 (5 = weak/thin SERPs a data-rich local page should beat; 1 = locked up by strong platforms/brands).

**Tool limitation (applies to every section below):** WebSearch approximates organic results and does not show ads or the Google local pack/map results — beatability here refers to organic content results only. The tool is US-localized, so Toronto queries reflect a US vantage, and some queries surfaced fewer or more than 8 results; classifications cover only what was actually returned. Result mix on live Google (with local pack + ads) will be more competitive for "near me" terms than shown here.
