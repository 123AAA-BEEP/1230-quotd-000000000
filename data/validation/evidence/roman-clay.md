- Person-form: industry uses "applicator" (American Clay's directory is literally "Find An Applicator": https://www.americanclay.com/texas); installers use "installation" language ("Roman Clay Installation & Restoration Services": https://www.romanclayfinishes.com/services). ## Assessment

Real, young, DIY-skewed trend. The find-a-pro gap is validated at industry level (manufacturer applicator directories) and supply is visibly metro-only, but roman clay lacks the cost-site infrastructure venetian plaster has, and its flagship content teaches DIY. Best treated as a companion page cluster to venetian plaster/limewash (consumers actively compare all three) rather than a standalone volume driver. Publish where data exists; expect scarce-market treatment often.
