## Assessment

Proven, steadily monetized niche — enough that two vertical aggregators and dedicated Thumbtack/Angi categories already exist. Demand driver is durable (aging pool stock, repair fatigue, insurance/liability), ticket is mid-high ($4k–$16k inground), and homeowner anxiety centers on trustworthy quotes — good lead-capture fit. But it is the most already-served niche in this batch, and industry momentum currently favors remodeling over removal.
