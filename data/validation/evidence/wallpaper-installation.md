- Supply-scarcity is instead documented statistically: BLS OEWS May 2023 estimates only ~1,830 employed paperhangers in the entire US, mean wage $25.97/hr / $54,020/yr (source: https://www.bls.gov/oes/2023/may/oes472142.htm via search summary; corroborating salary aggregators: https://www.recruiter.com/salaries/paperhangers-salary/).
