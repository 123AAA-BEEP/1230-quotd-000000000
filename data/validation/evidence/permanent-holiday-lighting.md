## Volume hints

- Pricing anchors (well-corroborated across sources): **$20–$38 per linear foot installed**; JellyFish typically $3,000–$6,000 per home, ~$4,600 average (https://www.jellyfishlighting.com/blog/how-much-do-permanent-christmas-lights-cost, vendor); Gemstone $28–$34/ft, Trimlight $20–$30/ft (comparison pages above). DIY kits (Govee etc.) $2–$4/ft — a real substitution threat at the low end (https://centralfloridatrimlight.com/trimlight-vs-jellyfish-lighting/).
- Broad category estimate only: global Christmas lights & decorations market ~$8.1B (2023) → ~$10.9B (2031), ~4.1% CAGR — https://centralpros.com/holiday-lighting-decoration-market-trends-and-growth-prospects/ (secondary estimate; NOT permanent-specific). **No permanent-specific volume number found — flagged as thin.**
- Direction: multiple industry/vendor sources describe permanent systems as the fastest-growing segment of holiday lighting (https://onesmartlighting.net/growth-potential-and-opportunities-in-the-permanent-outdoor-holiday-lighting-market/, https://wonderlylights.com/blog/holiday-light-trends-for-the-2025-season/ — vendor sources, directional only).
- **Seasonality shape: flatter than string-light installation.** Research/purchase interest still spikes Oct–Dec with the holiday category (outdoor christmas lights normalized 100 in Dec 2024 per https://www.accio.com/business/xmas-lights-trends, secondary), but installs happen year-round and dealers sell shoulder-season ("be ready by November") — a smoother demand curve than the ~80%-in-November seasonal install niche.

## Assessment

High-ticket ($3k–$6k), fast-growing, brand-dealer-mediated category with no marketplace taxonomy and heavy consumer cross-shopping between 3–4 branded systems — a natural fit for neutral per-foot price comparison and multi-dealer quotes. Weaknesses: demand evidence is vendor-heavy, permanent-specific volume numbers don't exist publicly, and cheap DIY kits siphon the low end. Pairs operationally with christmas-light-installation (same providers, opposite seasonality).
