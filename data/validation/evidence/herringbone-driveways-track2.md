## Criterion 3 — Premium-tier identifiability (roster data module)

**Verdict: PASS, with one honest gap.** A public, badge-verifiable premium tier exists via manufacturer authorized-contractor programs and ICPI/CMHA certification; a city-filterable roster module is buildable. The gap: clay-paver brands run dealer locators, not installer certification — clay specialists must be inferred, not scraped.

- **Belgard (best source):** public Find-a-Contractor directory with city-level pages (e.g. https://www.belgard.com/find-contractor/ and https://www.belgard.com/find-contractor/new-york-city/ ). Authorized Contractors are vetted annually — proof of insurance, CMHA installation certification, workmanship warranty — and installs by them carry a homeowner warranty (program terms: https://www.belgard.com/professionals/authorized-contractor-program/ , https://www.belgard.com/belgard-authorized-program/ ). Houzz mirrors Belgard authorized lists ( https://www.houzz.com/dealers/belgard/p/75 ), confirming the data is public.
- **Unilock:** Find-a-Contractor at https://unilock.com/find-contractor/ ; Authorized Contractor program checks homeowner references, verifies liability insurance and workers' comp, and backs installs with a 2-year workmanship guarantee ( https://contractor.unilock.com/business-support/become-an-authorized-contractor/ , https://unilock.com/how-to-choose-a-contractor/ ). Caveat: the finder is partly form/matching-based (call 1-800-UNILOCK or submit form) rather than a fully browsable list — scrapeability to verify.
- **Techo-Bloc:** operates city landing pages tied to its contractor network (e.g. https://www.techo-bloc.com/pavers-in-toronto ).
- **ICPI/CMHA certification:** the industry credential (25,000+ installers through the program since 1994: https://www.lawnandlandscape.com/article/icpi-notebook/ ; program: https://educate.icpi.org/contractors-installers ). Contractors market the badge prominently ( https://westernpavers.com/about/certifications/ , https://www.pavertime.com/icpi-certified , https://www.aschardscape.com/icpi-certified-installer/ ), and the City of Ottawa's rebate program **requires** an ICPI-certified installer (see Criterion 4) — municipal endorsement that the tier is real. **Unverified this session:** whether the post-merger CMHA (masonryandhardscapes.org) public installer directory is browsable/city-filterable — must be confirmed before the roster module is committed.
- **Clay-specific gap:** Pine Hall Brick (nation's largest clay-paver producer: https://pinehallbrick.com/clay-pavers/ ) and Glen-Gery surface dealers/suppliers (e.g. https://www.pennstone.com/hardscaping/brick-pavers/ ), not certified installers. No public clay-paver installer certification program found. A clay-specialist roster would be assembled from ICPI/CMHA + manufacturer-authorized lists + portfolio evidence, which is defensible but is editorial work, not a scrape.

## Criterion 4 — Data-module richness (does city data change the advice?)

**Verdict: PASS — strongest criterion.** Regulation, rebates, permits, and frost engineering all vary by city and materially change spec, cost, and even which contractor is eligible.

- **Toronto (regulation changes what you may build):** Municipal Code Chapter 918 — a front-yard parking pad must be paved with permeable paving material (defined as minimizing runoff to the sewer system), pad width limited to 2.2–2.6 m (3.66 m for accessible-permit holders), licensing process applies. Official: https://www.toronto.ca/legdocs/municode/1184_918.pdf ; city service page: https://www.toronto.ca/home/311-toronto-at-your-service/find-service-information/article/?kb=kA06g000001cvfhCAA ; Zoning By-law 569-2013 soft-landscaping percentages interact (plain-language guides: https://www.greenapple.ca/resources/the-ultimate-guide-to-torontos-permeable-paver-parking-rules , https://jgcontractingyyz.com/toronto-driveway-widening-and-front-yard-parking-2025-guide/ ). Market data to pair: permeable interlock $24–38/sq ft vs standard $16–25; min 6" compacted Granular A base, 8" on poor (clay) subgrades ( https://khanscapes.ca/interlocking-driveway-cost-toronto/ , https://countryreno.com/interlock-driveway-installation-toronto/ ).
- **Ottawa (rebate changes cost AND contractor choice):** Rain Ready Ottawa pays rebates up to $5,000 for rainwater projects including permeable pavement; permeable projects **must be installed by an ICPI-certified contractor or certified Fusion Landscape Professional**, sized to STEP LID guidelines, in priority areas checked by an address tool. Official: https://ottawa.ca/en/living-ottawa/environment-conservation-and-climate/protecting-ottawas-waterways/rain-ready-ottawa/rain-ready-ottawa-rebates and https://ottawa.ca/en/living-ottawa/environment-conservation-and-climate/protecting-ottawas-waterways/rain-ready-ottawa/rain-ready-ottawa-residential-rebates . This single module ties rebate money to our certified-installer roster — the cleanest possible Track-2 data story.
- **Chicago (permits + stormwater code):** CDOT driveway permit required for installing a driveway, with residential policy (properties ≤4 units; $250,000 insurance requirement): https://www.chicago.gov/city/en/depts/cdot/supp_info/driveway_permits/overview_and_policy.html (FAQ: https://www.chicago.gov/city/en/depts/cdot/supp_info/driveway_permits/frequently_askedquestions.html ). DWM Stormwater Management Ordinance Manual (Aug 2024) treats permeable pavers as a stormwater BMP with slope requirements: https://www.chicago.gov/content/dam/city/depts/water/general/Engineering/SewerConstStormReq/2024StormwaterManual.pdf ; 2026 sewer regulations: https://www.chicago.gov/content/dam/city/depts/water/general/Engineering/SewerConstStormReq/2026%20Regulations.pdf
- **Frost line → excavation/base spec → cost (varies by city):** driveway excavation runs 10–18" total; in frost-susceptible regions add 2–3" to base depth, and the standard engineering approach is base extending to at least half the local design frost depth (24" frost → 12" base): https://ernestmaier.com/how-deep-should-your-paver-base-be-a-guide-for-patios/ , https://prolandscapesmd.com/ultimate-freeze-thaw-safe-paver-installation-guide/ , https://mquip.com/how-deep-do-you-need-to-excavate-before-laying-pavers/ , https://gappsi.com/2026/07/28/paver-driveway-installation-guide/ . Since design frost depth is a per-city figure, excavation volume — the largest cost driver — is genuinely city-varying advice, and freeze-thaw regions additionally need polymeric-sand/edge-restraint discipline (per the freeze-thaw guide above and Toronto base specs).
