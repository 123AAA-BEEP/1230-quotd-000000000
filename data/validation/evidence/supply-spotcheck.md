## Cross-cutting observations (P0-relevant)

1. **Standalone sites dominate.** In every cell, ≥3 providers had their own websites with niche-specific service pages. Directories (Yelp, HomeAdvisor, BBB, TaskRabbit, TrustedPros, teamdavelogan.com) appeared in SERPs but were never the only source. Lead buyers exist everywhere in this grid.
2. **These niches are already industrialized in places.** Franchise/multi-city brands: That 1 Painter + LIME Painting (limewash, multiple metros), Shack Shine + We Hang Christmas Lights (holiday lighting, multiple metros), SWAT Environmental + Protect Environmental (radon, all three US metros). Sophisticated buyers who already pay for demand — good for lead sales, but SERPs are contested.
3. **Programmatic SEO competitors are already in these SERPs:** the "French Drains {City}" exact-match-domain network (Chicago/Denver/Atlanta, shared template + founder story), jensonbricklimewashing.com and limewashinghouse.com (templated city pages, limewash), findradonpros.com (radon directory, "183 contractors" claims), venetianplastershop.ca ("best contractors in Toronto 2026" listicles). The niches are unusual; the tactic is not.
4. **Toronto radon is the marginal cell** — first-pass SERP was certification bodies + a directory; specialists exist (Mr. Radon, RadonSafeToronto, Radon Gas Guys) but the bench is short. Watch Canadian radon cells for scarce-market treatment at city granularity (Toronto ≠ all of Ontario; C-NRPP registry can seed prospects).
5. **Denver slate** clears on page evidence but is mostly general roofers with slate service lines, not dedicated slate houses — lead quality expectations should differ from Chicago/Toronto where century-old slate specialists exist.
6. **Niche-named exact-match domains recur** (limewashchicago.com, tpvenetianplaster.com, frenchdrainsdenver.com, christmaslights.to, 5280radonmitigation.com): providers themselves chase this exact search demand, which is direct evidence they value these leads.
