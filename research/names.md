# Name Candidates — Specialty Services Platform

> **DECIDED 2026-08-01: the chosen name is `GetQuotd` / brand "Quotd" at `getquotd.com`** (owner's pick, from outside this list). This document is retained as the naming record and for future sub-brand needs.

*151 unique candidates from a 4-lens brainstorm (scarcity, craft mastery, colloquial, specialty-literal) plus a curated batch. DNS-checked 2026-07-31: "likely available" = no NS/A records found — a strong signal but NOT proof; verify at a registrar before falling in love, and run a USPTO/CIPO trademark screen on the finalist. Premium-looking "available" names (doneright.com, askaround.com) are probably parked or premium-priced — treat with extra suspicion.*
