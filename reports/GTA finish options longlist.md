# GTA finish options longlist

A wide screen of specialty finishes and finish-adjacent services for the Greater Toronto Area, built for a review pass. The goal is breadth, not depth: every plausible "next limewash" candidate is here, including ones that fail, so you can see why they fail.

**How to read the columns**

- **Ticket**: typical job size in CAD for a GTA homeowner. "Fit" means it lands naturally in $5k–15k. Prices marked † are from the research notes (contractor-published); everything else is my estimate.
- **Stage**: where the trend is on its curve in Canada. Early = designers only; Rising = consumers searching by name, few pros; Peak = widely known, pros multiplying; Mature = established trade; Fading = past peak; ? = no usable data.
- **GTA pros**: rough count of credible specialists marketing it by name. Counts marked † come from the research notes.
- **Adjacency**: how close it is to limewash skills, tools, suppliers and clientele. High = same trowel/brush/clientele; Med = new product, same customer; Low = different trade.
- **Season**: I = interior (fills Nov–Apr); E = exterior (competes with limewash's late-May to early-Oct window); I/E = both.
- **Grade**: A = serious candidate; B = worth a closer look or as a line extension; C = fails on ticket, trend, or trade mismatch; X = avoid.

Rows marked ★ were covered in depth in the research report; the rest are screened from general market knowledge and need verification before you act on them.

**Pattern across the A and B rows:** nearly all are interior, trowel-applied, lime- or cement-based, and sold to the same designer-led clientele. That is one winter practice, not twenty businesses. The exterior rows add revenue in the season you already sell; the interior plaster rows add the seven months you do not.

**What this list is not:** verified. Rows without † are screened from general market knowledge. Before committing to any row, run the two checks from the main report: a Toronto-targeted keyword pull and a Maps/HomeStars census of who already sells it.
