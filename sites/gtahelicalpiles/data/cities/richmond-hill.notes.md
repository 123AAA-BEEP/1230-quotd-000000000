# Richmond Hill local-data module — research notes

Retrieved 2026-11-03. Companion to `richmond-hill.json`. Every figure in the JSON traces to a URL listed in its `sources` array; anything that could not be retrieved is `null` with the reason below. Richmond Hill is a City (since March 25, 2019) in York Region; CSD 3519038, DGUID 2021A00053519038.

## What was verified (with source host)

**Coordinates**: the module coordinate is Wikipedia's infobox coordinate for Richmond Hill, 43°52′29″N 79°26′22″W = 43.87472, −79.43944 (cited there to the Canadian Geographical Names Database, FCKYR). It falls on Yonge Street in the old village between Crosby Avenue and Major Mackenzie Drive. Infobox elevation 233 m; land area 100.79 km² (2021).

The 55-unit difference in the single-detached count against the ward profile is a rounding/vintage difference; the JSON uses the ward-profile figure.
  - Wikipedia (citing the Census Profile): 202,022 people in 69,314 of 72,017 total private dwellings; 2016 population 195,022 (+3.6 %, derived); historical series 2001 132,030; 2006 162,704; 2011 185,541.

Fill from the Census Profile when it is reachable.

  - Points other than the Wikipedia ones are my approximations placed inside the road boundaries Wikipedia gives (Jefferson: Gamble Rd–Bathurst–Bayview; Headford: Major Mackenzie–Leslie–404–16th; Elgin Mills: Yonge from Gamble to Elgin Mills Rd; Bayview Hill: NE of Bayview and 16th; Richvale: Yonge–Bathurst either side of Carrville; Doncrest: east of Yonge north of Highway 7). They are not surveyed centroids. OGS attributes are quoted verbatim.
- Surficial results: **stone-poor sandy silt to silty sand till (unit 5b)** at the reference, Yonge/Major Mackenzie, Mill Pond, Elgin Mills, Headford (one polygon, OBJECTID 88109) and Gormley (OBJECTID 87533); **clay-to-silt till (unit 5d)** at Yonge south of Crosby (88951), Jefferson and north of Gormley (84470), Langstaff (89413); **glaciofluvial river and delta deposits (unit 7)** at both Oak Ridges points and both Lake Wilcox points (86789); **fine-textured glaciolacustrine silt and clay, massive to well laminated (unit 8a)** at Bayview Hill, Richvale, Doncrest and Yonge/Highway 7 (88780); **interbedded silt, clay and gritty pebbly flow till (unit 8b)** at Bond Lake (87118). No point returned unit 3 (Paleozoic bedrock at or near surface).
- Bedrock results: **Blue Mountain Formation** (OGS unit 12; original formation name "Whitby", original lithology "Collingwood Member: black shale"; dark blue-grey to brown to black shale with thin limestone/calcareous siltstone interbeds) at the reference, Yonge/Major Mackenzie, Mill Pond, Yonge south of Crosby, Elgin Mills, Headford, both Gormley points, both Lake Wilcox points, Bayview Hill and Richvale; **Georgian Bay Formation** (unit 14) at Doncrest, Langstaff and Yonge/Highway 7; **Lindsay Formation, Simcoe Group** (unit 11; nodular argillaceous limestone; Collingwood upper member black fissile limestone) at both Oak Ridges points, Bond Lake and Jefferson. Source map m2544 throughout. Richmond Hill is the first city in this data set with three bedrock formations and the first with Blue Mountain or Lindsay at any point.
- Wikipedia (Richmond Hill): on the Oak Ridges Moraine, which makes up roughly the northern third of the city's land area; porous soil feeding aquifers; "very high water table, which poses some problems to construction"; streams drain south to Lake Ontario or north to Lake Simcoe; Rouge, Humber and Don receive flow from sources in the city. Wikipedia (Oak Ridges): wholly on the moraine, most of the area above 300 m, kettle lakes Lake Wilcox, Bond Lake, Philips Lake, Swan Lake, Lake St. George. Wikipedia (Lake Wilcox): 55.6 ha, 17.4 m deep, largest kettle lake on the moraine, drains to the East Humber. LSRCA watershed page: "The Oak Ridges Moraine is the southern watershed divide – lands drain north off the moraine to Lake Simcoe"; watershed municipalities include York Region.

**Permits (richmondhill.ca; forms.richmondhill.ca)**
- The City relaunched its website; the `/en/register-apply-pay/…` URLs 404. Live pages: Building Permits `/en/online-services/Building-Permits.aspx` (the `/en/register-apply-or-pay/Building-Permits.aspx` link on the home page 301s to it), Residential Permits `/en/online-services/Residential-Permits.aspx`, Building Inspections `/en/online-services/Building-Inspections.aspx`, Building Permit FAQs `/en/online-services/Building-Permit-FAQs.aspx`.
- Deck checklist **R6 "Deck Permit Application Process"** (PDF, fillable form): permit required where the deck is attached to a building, is more than 600 mm (2 ft) above average grade, or is greater than 10 m² (108 ft²); "$218.46 FLAT FEE"; decks subject to Zoning By-law setbacks; the OBC does not allow attachment to brick veneer. Required drawings include "Foundation - size, spacing (minimum depth 1200 mm / 4 ft. below grade & to undisturbed soil)". Pre-approvals where applicable: executed Site Plan Agreement, Heritage Richmond Hill, "T.R.C.A. (for properties within regulated area)". Two copies, one if digital. Fees "as per By-Law 19-24". Schedule 1 BCIN or architect/engineer stamp where applicable; homeowner may draw.
- **No helical pile wording anywhere.** The R6 checklist, the R1/R2 checklists and the four City pages retrieved do not mention helical or screw piles, alternative foundations, or an engineer requirement for them; the only CCMC reference is "Plastic guards are not permitted unless approved by CCMC". The R1 and R2 checklists list "Soils Report by a Professional Engineer (dependent on location of site)" and R2 lists "Underpinning details".
- Department name as printed: **"Building Division"** within "Planning and Building Services" (FAQ: "The Planning and Building Services, Building Division receives applications for and issues building permits"; checklists: "Building Division, 3rd floor - 905-771-8810 - building@richmondhill.ca"); inspections by the "Building Inspection Section" (buildinginspections@richmondhill.ca; 905-771-5465 on the Residential Project Guidelines). Address 225 East Beaver Creek Road, 3rd Floor, L4B 3P4.
- Portal: "Building Permit - Apply and Pay Online" links to `https://permits.richmondhill.ca/Energov_Prod/SelfService#/applicationAssistant…` (Tyler EnerGov self-service). permits.richmondhill.ca reset the connection from this environment, so the portal itself was not opened; the JSON records the base path. All residential permit types are online; two-step pre-screening then formal application; fee by Visa/Mastercard; formal submission within two weeks of the confirmation email; flattened unprotected black-and-white PDFs; no scans.
- Inspections page: issued permit required to book; requests after 3 p.m. count as next business day; Service Charter: mandatory-inspection requests answered within 2 business days; owner books each stage; no occupancy before final inspection. Booking via the Building Inspection Request form on forms.richmondhill.ca.
- Fees (R1, R2, Residential Permits page): detached/semi/townhouse/duplex and additions $27.37 per m²; alteration $10.40 per m²; finished basement $6.56 per m²; minimum residential $210.12; basement walkout $210.12; plumbing $158.40 minimum, $47.97 water service, $196.61 sanitary/storm drains, $28.40 per fixture (additions/alterations); SFD demolition $546.14. Deposits: grading $7,000 and road damage/frontage $3,000 for new houses and for additions over 400 ft² footprint (which also need a Site Alteration Permit). Fees over $20,000 may be split 50/50 between application and issuance.
- Conservation authorities: every City document retrieved names only the **TRCA** (checklist pre-approval; Residential Permits page "Conservation authority approval (TRCA)"; Zoning By-law 93-25 Overlay "E" TRCA Regulation Area and Hazardous Lands; Lake Wilcox SPA based on TRCA floodplain mapping). The LSRCA's pages do not name Richmond Hill, but say the Lake Simcoe watershed lies partly in York Region and that lands drain north off the Oak Ridges Moraine to Lake Simcoe, and that development in its Regulated Area may need a permit under O. Reg. 41/24. The JSON states that split exactly; see "NOT verified".

**Climate (climate.weather.gc.ca)**
- A **Richmond Hill** station exists in the 1981–2010 set: Climate ID 6157012, 43°52′38.094″N 79°26′52.074″W, 240 m, WMO-standard flag; 0.7 km WNW of the reference coordinate (derived). All elements computed from 1981–2006 (26 years, 1 missing, code A); frost dates from 25 years. January: max −2.2, mean −6.2, min −10.2 °C; extreme minimum −32.5 °C; **40.9 days/yr with minimum < −10 °C**; 58.0 days/yr with maximum ≤ 0 °C; 144.4 days/yr with minimum ≤ 0 °C (so about 86 freeze-thaw days by subtraction); snowfall 150.6 cm; precipitation 895.2 mm; last spring frost May 05, first fall frost Oct 10, **frost-free period 157 days**.
- **Toronto Pearson** 1991–2020 (6158731, 173.4 m, 26.8 km SW): January mean −5.0 °C, min −8.9 °C; 34.2 days < −10 °C; 49.5 days max ≤ 0 °C; 130.6 days min ≤ 0 °C; 114.5 cm snow; frost-free 174 days (Apr 27–Oct 20). Richmond Hill is colder, snowier and has a 17-day shorter frost-free season, though the two periods differ.
- **Toronto Buttonville A** (6158409, 198.1 m, 5.9 km ESE): the 1981–2010 results page returned station metadata and the legend but no element rows (the page has no data tables), so nothing from it is used.
- Pitfall avoided: the first "≤ 0 °C" row on the page is under *Days with Maximum Temperature* (58.0) and the second under *Days with Minimum Temperature* (144.4); the cold-day count is the *Minimum* "< −10 °C" row (40.9).
- The `station_select_1991_2020` route returned an empty results body for "richmond" and "toronto" in this session (the 1981–2010 route worked), so the absence of a Richmond Hill station from the 1991–2020 set is inferred from the Milton notes' earlier full read of that list, not re-verified today.

**Code (publications.gov.on.ca)**: the Ministry's free-download 2024 Building Code Compendium PDF (O. Reg. 163/24, 16.6 MB) was retrieved and text-extracted. Table 9.12.2.2. gives 1.2 m for clay or soils not clearly defined under a heated basement, and 1.2 m but not less than the depth of frost penetration with no heated space; rock and coarse-grained soils with good drainage "No limit"; 9.12.2.1.(1) excavation to undisturbed soil; 9.12.2.2.(5) lesser depths by local experience or design. Also read for the ICF page: 9.15.4.1.(1) ICF units conform to CAN/ULC-S717.1; 9.13.2.1 dampproofing; 9.14.2.1 foundation wall drainage, with (1.1) on insulation extending more than 900 mm below grade. `code.clause` is therefore filled, unlike Milton. ontario.ca/laws/regulation/120332 still returns a 54 KB JavaScript shell with no "9.12.2.2" in it.

**Cold-weather concreting (rmcao.org)**: Concrete Ontario's "Cold Weather Concreting" PDF (used on the Brampton ICF page) was re-fetched: CSA A23.1 cold weather is a probability of air temperature below 5 °C within 24 h of placing; minimum concrete temperature 10 °C for sections under 0.3 m (maximum 32 °C); 7 MPa before early frost.

## What was NOT verified (and why)

- **Period of construction.** `null` × 4; see Census above.
- **Census counts at source.** StatCan returned 403 to every request; the counts are from the City's ward profiles, York Region's report and Wikipedia, each of which cites the Census Profile. Confirm against StatCan when reachable.
- **Inspection stage names for housing.** The Building Inspections page has a "Housing projects" stage section inside a collapsed block whose text is not in the HTML served; `inspections` is an empty array with `inspections_note`. The ICF page therefore describes the Code's sequence (footing forms before the pour, dampproofing and drainage before backfill) as what we book, not as the City's published list.
- **Fee by-law 19-24 and its effective date.** The checklists and pages cite it but the schedule PDF was not found; the 2026 Tariff of Fees By-law 61-25 (effective January 1, 2026) was retrieved but its text covers development applications only. The $218.46 / $27.37 / $210.12 figures are as printed on the City's current pages and checklists, undated.
- **LSRCA jurisdiction inside Richmond Hill.** No City page names the Lake Simcoe Region Conservation Authority; its own pages say York Region and the moraine divide without naming the city. The content says TRCA clearance per the City's checklist and advises checking the LSRCA map viewer for a lot that drains north off the moraine; it does not assert a line. **Owner should confirm** with the Building Division whether any Richmond Hill lots fall in the LSRCA regulated area.
- **Online portal.** permits.richmondhill.ca reset the TCP connection; the URL is taken from the City's page link, not opened.
- **Depth to bedrock / drift thickness.** Not queried.
- **Buttonville normals.** No element rows served.
- **Deck guide drawings.** Richmond Hill publishes a checklist, not a sample drawing set; there is no City drawing with a pier detail, guard height or deck-block limit to quote.

## Generic template claims — do they hold for Richmond Hill?

| Template claim | Holds? | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| "Ontario's 4-foot frost line" | **Yes, as a municipal requirement and as Code** | R6 checklist: "minimum depth 1200 mm / 4 ft. below grade & to undisturbed soil"; 2024 Compendium Table 9.12.2.2. gives 1.2 m for clay or soils not clearly defined. Cite both. |
| "Queenston shale" | **No** | No Richmond Hill point returns Queenston. Bedrock is Blue Mountain Formation shale under the centre, Georgian Bay shale under the south (Doncrest, Langstaff) and Lindsay limestone under the moraine in the north-west (Oak Ridges, Bond Lake, Jefferson). Say "Blue Mountain shale under most of the city; limestone under the moraine". |
| "[City] Building Department" | **Use "Building Division"** | Printed on every checklist and the FAQ ("Planning and Building Services, Building Division"); inspections by the "Building Inspection Section". "Building Department" does not appear. |
| "36-inch gates" | **Not a municipal requirement; do not present it as one** | No Richmond Hill page or checklist mentions a gate width. If the site's gate line is about rig access it must be sourced to the contractor's own equipment specifications. |
| "Deck permit above 24 inches" | **Yes, but it is one of three triggers** | 600 mm above average grade, or attached to a building, or over 10 m² (R6). Unlike Toronto (height only) and Milton (area or attachment, no height), Richmond Hill prints all three. |

Also worth noting for copy: the Richmond Hill climate station is the closest station to any city centre in this data set (0.7 km) and the coldest, snowiest record (150.6 cm) used so far; the Oak Ridges Moraine points are the only glaciofluvial sand-and-gravel (unit 7) results in the set.
